Refunds

Last updated: 3 October 2026

If turnslip Pro is not right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of buying it. No questions asked.

How to ask

Email support@turnslip.dev with the email address you bought with, or reply to the receipt email from Dodo Payments.

What happens

The refund goes back through Dodo Payments to the card or account you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it. Your license key stops working once the refund is issued.

After 14 days

If a defect keeps Pro from working on a supported setup and we cannot fix it, write to us: we look at each case.
