Privacy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This website

turnslip.dev sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another site. Its fonts are served from turnslip.dev itself.

After you pay, Dodo Payments sends you back to turnslip.dev/thanks with your order details, including your license key and email address in the page address. The page is static: nothing on it reads, stores or forwards them, but like any address they reach Cloudflare and stay in your browser history.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the pages you request, and keeps request logs for security. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The plugin

turnslip keeps its snapshots, slips and history in ~/.turnslip on your machine. It sends no telemetry. It never sends your files, your prompts or Claude's replies anywhere.

turnslip Pro sends your license key and an activation id for your machine to Dodo Payments when you activate, about once a week to check the key, and when you deactivate. Nothing else is sent.

Payments

Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, collects and processes your payment details, name, email and country under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address, your country and the order details so we can support you. We never see your card number.

Email

If you write to support@turnslip.dev, we keep your messages as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than two years.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, by emailing support@turnslip.dev. Data on your own machine is yours: delete ~/.turnslip to remove it.
