Thank you for buying turnslip Pro

Dodo Payments is emailing your receipt and your license key to the address you paid with. It usually arrives within a few minutes; if it does not, look in your spam folder.

Activate Pro

In Claude Code, run:

/turnslip:activate <key>

Paste the key from the email in place of <key>.

One key works on up to 3 machines; free one with /turnslip:deactivate.

Not installed yet? Follow the install steps first.

Something wrong?

No email after an hour, or the key does not activate: write to support@turnslip.dev with the address you paid with. Not what you wanted: you can get a full refund within 14 days, see Refunds.
